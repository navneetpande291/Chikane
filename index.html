The line is everything

Chicane's handling model is built around one idea: every corner has a line, and only one of them is fast. Late braking, an early apex, a greedy throttle — each costs you time somewhere else on the lap.

Tyres wear per compound and per corner. Tracks evolve as rubber goes down across a session. There's no rewind, no racing line overlay, and no assist that finds the apex for you.
